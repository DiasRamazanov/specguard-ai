## Release blockers (owner / Codex)
1. **R1:** push or recover the release, deployment and Railway work. Nothing is on the remote.
2. **R2:** merge `feat/core-engine` into `main` (clean merge verified) and tag the commit that produced the official report.
3. **R3:** decide on repository visibility or jury access. Check the rights to the reference images first.
4. Put the official report and the issued input together for submission. Both are outside Git.
5. Confirm the submission channel and the deadline timezone with the organizers.
6. Optional: record a short demo video as a fallback. Not required by the site.

## Must not happen
- Do not show recorded results as a new live inference; label them «сохранённый прогон от 26.11.2026».
- Do not claim accuracy above the measured 5/9 without a new evaluation.
- Do not present 100% citation verification as 100% semantic accuracy.
- Do not enable `SPECGUARD_LIVE=1` on a public URL.
